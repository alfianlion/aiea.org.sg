About AIEA

Built for Singapore automobile importers and exporters.

AIEA was officially registered on 11 January 2000 to represent members of Singapore's automotive import, export, parallel import, and motor trade community.

An association shaped by the trade

From member communication to industry standards, AIEA provides a practical platform for Singapore automotive businesses.

What AIEA Does

Supporting Singapore's automobile import & export industry

AIEA represents Singapore automobile importers, exporters, parallel import dealers, and motor trade businesses through representation, authority liaison, trade standards, and member support.
