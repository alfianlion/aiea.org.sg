About AIEA

Representing Singapore's automobile import and export trade since 2000.

Automobile Importer & Exporter Association (Singapore)

Our History

Built from the trade, for the trade.

The initial idea to form an association of automobile importers and exporters was first mooted when a group of car dealers sat down for their regular lunch get-together in late 1998 to exchange ideas and share industry news.

At that time, rules and regulations pertaining to the parallel import of new cars were slowly being liberalised by the authorities and more players were entering the trade. Recognising the need for a unified voice, industry members came together to establish a formal body.

With the blessing of the National Environment Agency and the Land Transport Authority, the Automobile Importer & Exporter Association (Singapore) — AIEA — was officially registered with the Registrar of Societies on 11 January 2000 with ten founding members.

What We Stand For

Objectives of the Association

AIEA exists to serve and strengthen Singapore's automobile import and export community through six core commitments.

01

Protect member interests

Encourage, promote and protect the interests of all AIEA members within Singapore's automotive trade.

02

Liaise with authorities

Act as a liaison body and communication platform between relevant government bodies and members as a whole.

03

Organise industry activities

Organise activities and programmes to improve member expertise and services, upgrading the automobile trading industry as a whole.

04

Promote proper trade practices

Establish and promote just, transparent, and professional trade practices amongst all members.

05

Member communications

Act as an effective medium of communication between members, keeping the industry informed and connected.

06

Collect industry data

Collect information, data, and statistics relating to the automobile trade industry for the benefit of all members.

Governance

Governing Structure of the Association

AIEA operates under a transparent governance framework with clear roles at every level.

1

Articles of Incorporation & By-laws

The articles of incorporation constitute the basic rules and regulations regarding the organisation and operation of the association, forming the legal foundation of AIEA's structure.

2

General Meeting

The General Meeting, which consists of all members of the association, is the highest decision-making body. Key decisions affecting the association and its membership are made here.

3

Executive Committee

The Executive Committee is the decision-making body for the enforcement of association business. It comprises the President, Vice President, Hon. Secretary, Hon. Treasurer, and up to 7 Ordinary Committee Members.
